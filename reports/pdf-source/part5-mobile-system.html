Touch and typing on phones

  • Targets: WCAG minimum 24 px; the app uses 44 px buttons and inputs, 48 px list rows and 64 px bottom-bar items on touch (pointer-coarse: variant); desktop keeps shadcn's density.
  • 16 px input text so iPhones don't zoom.
  • Numbers: inputmode="decimal"; phones: type="tel"; vehicle and GSTIN: capitals automatically.
  • Keyboard "Next" key moves between fields; "Done" on the last one.
  • Primary action in a sticky bottom bar, clear of the phone's gesture area (safe-area-inset).
  • Destructive actions live in ⋯ menus, never next to Save.
  • Things used most in the yard (the "+" button, Save) sit in the thumb zone at the bottom.
  • No pull-to-refresh, no swipe-only actions: every gesture has a button alternative.

The click reducers, by impact

  • "Deliver" where the decision is made: no PO picking
  • Quantity defaults to the maximum; "Use max" in every limit error
  • Predictable defaults, tagged "Last used", always editable
  • Inline "+ Add '…'" in every picker (Alt+C, as in Tally)

  • Filters in the URL: no re-filtering after Back
  • Sales Orders opens on "Open"
  • Keyboard-first: Enter to next field, Ctrl+Enter to save
  • Centre "+" on phones: any transaction in two taps

Consistency guard-rails

  • Edits to components/ui are limited to added variants and accessibility fixes, logged in CHANGES.md; updates via shadcn add --diff.
  • Lint bans raw colour classes (bg-red-500) and direct imports of @base-ui/react / sonner in features.
  • A dev-only /dev/components page shows every composite in every state: cheaper than Storybook for a small team.
  • All numbers through lib/format.ts; all toasts through lib/notify.ts; all permissions through can('write').

Most of this guide rests on established research and design-system guidance. A few choices are reasoned rather than tested, and a few need small backend additions.

Validate with real users before building everything

  • Run a short session with 3–5 people (owner, accountant, yard staff) on a mid-range Android phone in daylight, using the delivery form and one filtered list.
  • Check: click counts, whether hiding (rather than disabling) buttons for VIEWERs confuses anyone, and whether the trade words ("Left to deliver", "Stock from") read naturally.
  • Confirm the Sunlight theme is readable outdoors and the 44 px targets are comfortable with work gloves.

Still to verify

  • Contrast of every proposed colour pair (tweakcn) in light, dark and Sunlight themes.
  • Vehicle-number and GSTIN patterns against official formats.
  • That the shadcn data-table guide and chosen community table code work with TanStack Table v9.
